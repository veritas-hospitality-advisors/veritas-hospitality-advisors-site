## Performance Concerns
- ~25% of hotels report occupancies ≤50% (Hotelivate 2025)
- Approx. 25% of hotels are in bottom performance quartile
- New hotels (post-2020/21): First-year occupancy only 40–45%
- Established hotels (pre-2020/21): Occupancy 71.9%, ADR ₹8,701 — outperforming by 3.9 pts
- Supply growth (32% since 2019) roughly matching demand growth (30%) — occupancy flat despite strong rate growth
