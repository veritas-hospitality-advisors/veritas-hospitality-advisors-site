## GST Structure for Indian Hotels (Current as of 2025)

### Room Accommodation GST Rates
| Room Tariff Per Night | GST Rate | ITC Available? |
|-----------------------|----------|---------------|
| Less than ₹1,000 | 12% | No |
| ₹1,000 to ₹7,500 | 12% | No |
| Above ₹7,500 | 18% | Yes |

**Key note:** "Declared Tariff" includes all amenities charges (furniture, AC, refrigerators) minus published discounts. Seasonal rate variations: rate applicable at the time of supply governs.

**Update effective 22 September 2025 (ClearTax):**
- ≤ ₹7,500: 5% GST (ITC not available)
- > ₹7,500: 18% GST (ITC available)
- **Note:** There is some discrepancy between sources on whether ₹1,000–7,500 bracket is 12% (older) or 5% (newer). The 5% rate for ≤₹7,500 reflects a significant reduction from the previous 12% for mid-range hotels.

### Restaurant & F&B GST
| Service Type | GST Rate | ITC |
|--------------|----------|-----|
| Standalone restaurants | 5% | No |
| Hotel restaurant — hotel tariff <₹7,500 | 5% | No |
| Hotel restaurant — hotel tariff ≥₹7,500 ("specified premises") | 18% | Yes |
| Banquets/events with food | 18% | Yes |
| Outdoor catering | 5% | No |

**"Specified premises" definition:** Hotels where at least one room exceeded ₹7,500 tariff threshold in the previous financial year.

### Additional Services
| Service | GST Rate |
|---------|----------|
| Spa, laundry, beauty services | 18% |
| Alcoholic beverages | Exempt from GST; State VAT applies |
| Foreign currency exchange | 18% |

### Composition Scheme
- Restaurants with turnover below ₹1.5 crore may elect 5% GST (2.5% CGST + 2.5% SGST)
- No ITC recovery once scheme elected

### ITC Reversal Complexity
Hotels operating rooms in both tariff brackets (≤₹7,500 and >₹7,500) must reverse ITC on common inputs proportionally based on turnover under GST Rule 42 — a significant compliance burden for mid-market properties operating across room types.

### Practical Impact on Hotel Owners
- Mid-market hotels charging ₹5,000–7,500/night face GST at lower rate but cannot claim ITC — effectively an unrecoverable tax on inputs
- Luxury hotels (>₹7,500) can recover ITC, making their cost base more competitive
- F&B revenue from hotel restaurants that cross the "specified premises" threshold faces 18% GST, potentially deterring walk-in restaurant guests
- Alcohol (often 30–40% of F&B revenue in leisure/full-service hotels) is NOT under GST; state excise and VAT applies creating complex compliance

## OTA Commission Rates in India (2024-2026)

### Platform-by-Platform Standard Rates
| Platform | Standard Commission | Preferred/Premium | Notes |
|----------|--------------------|--------------------|-------|
| MakeMyTrip | 18–22% | 22–28% | Plus seasonal sale participation, co-marketing fees |
| Goibibo | 18–22% | 22–28% | GoCash rewards affect net; flash sales mandatory |
| Booking.com | 15–18% | 18–25% | Genius discounts (10–15%), mobile deal costs |
| Agoda | 15–20% | 20–25% | Flexible payment models, aggressive pricing |
| Expedia | 15–20% | 20–25% | Package deals, rewards program costs |
| Yatra | 16–20% | 20–24% | Corporate travel focus, bulk booking options |
| EaseMyTrip | 12–18% | 18–22% | Lower rates, growing network |
| Cleartrip | 15–20% | 20–24% | Flipkart ecosystem integration |
| Airbnb | 3% (host) + 14% (guest) | Variable | Split-fee; ~17% total; not typical for hotels |
| OYO | 25–35% revenue share | N/A | Full operational support included |

**Full OTA commission formula (India):** 15–25% + 18% GST on commission + TDS 0.1% + TCS 0.5%
**MakeMyTrip/Goibibo combined dominant share:** ~60–65% of Indian online hotel bookings

### True Total Cost of OTA Distribution
Beyond stated commissions, effective total cost reaches 25–45% when including:
- Mandatory promotional discounts: 10–20%
- Preferred program fees: +3–7%
- Payment processing: 1–3%
- Cancellation costs (flexible policy requirements)
- Rate parity clause compliance cost (can't undercut OTA price on own website)

### Payment Timing Issue
OTA payment to hotels: 15–45 days after checkout — significant cash flow strain vs. direct booking (instant UPI transfer).

### Indian OTA Market Structure
- MakeMyTrip (owns Goibibo): Dominant in domestic leisure travel
- Booking.com: Strong in international traveller segment
- Agoda: Growing in price-sensitive segments
- OYO: Budget-to-midscale franchise model
- Yatra: Strong in corporate segment

### OTA Dependency Financial Model (Illustrative — apycue.com)
10-room property, 60% occupancy, ₹2,500/night rate:
- Annual revenue: ~₹54.75 lakh
- At 80% OTA dependency with 20% commission: Loss = ₹8.76 lakh annually
- Shift to 40% OTA dependency: Saves ₹4.38 lakh annually — enough for property upgrades

## Energy Costs for Indian Hotels

### Sector-Wide Data (Energetica India, 2024)
- India hospitality sector summer energy bill: ₹7,400 crore total
- Projected electricity consumption: 9.35 billion units (10% YoY increase)
- Average commercial electricity tariff: ₹8 per unit
- AC, refrigeration, water heating: 60–70% of total hotel power consumption
- Diesel generator costs: ₹50,000–1 lakh/month (metro properties, depending on power reliability)

### Property-Level Energy Bills
| Property Size | Monthly Energy Bill |
|---------------|---------------------|
| 30-room metro property | ₹1.5–3 lakh/month |
| 50-room property | ₹2.5–5 lakh/month |

### Regional Electricity Cost Increases (Summer 2024)
- Delhi: 25–30% spike
- Hyderabad: 20–25% increase
- Mumbai: 15–20% rise
- Bangalore: 10–15% growth

### Energy as % of Revenue — Best Available Benchmarks
From 2016-17 HVS-FHRAI Survey (still directionally relevant):
- Overall range: 6.9–10.4% of revenue
- 5-Star Deluxe: 8.2%
- 5-Star: 10.4% (higher F&B/amenities energy loads)
- 4-Star: 6.9%
- 3-Star: 7.1%
- Budget: 9.3%
- All-India average: ~8.5%

**Current estimate 2024–25:** Likely 8–12% of revenue for mid-market hotels given tariff increases. Some sources suggest 3–5% of revenue for highly efficient properties; the 8–12% range is more representative for independent/older hotels without energy management systems.

Solar adoption saving 30–50% on electricity bills; ITC Maratha meets 75% of electricity demand from renewables.
AI-driven energy management saving 20–25% (₹30,000–50,000/month for mid-sized hotels).
