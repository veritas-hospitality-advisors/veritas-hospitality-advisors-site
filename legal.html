1. Privacy Policy

Veritas Hospitality Advisors ("Veritas", "we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting the personal data of all individuals who visit our website (veritashospitalityadvisors.in) or contact us through it. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and your rights in relation to it.

This policy is governed by and compliant with:

  • The Information Technology Act, 2000 (IT Act) and the IT (Amendment) Act, 2008
  • The Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011
  • The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), to the extent its provisions are in force

Data Fiduciary: For the purposes of the DPDP Act 2023, Veritas Hospitality Advisors is the Data Fiduciary in respect of personal data collected through this website.

2. Data we collect

2.1 Data you provide

We collect personal data only when you actively provide it. This includes:

  • Email address — when you submit the playbook download form or the Insights subscription form
  • Name — if included in any form or email you send us
  • Email content and enquiry details — when you contact us at vaishakh@veritashospitalityadvisors.in

2.2 Data collected automatically

When you visit our website, standard technical data is collected by our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) in server logs. If you click “Accept analytics” in the cookie banner, Google Analytics also collects data such as your IP address, browser type, device type and the pages you view (see Section 5, Cookies). Before you make a choice, the Google tag may send limited signals that do not use cookies. We use this data in aggregate and do not use it to identify individual users.

2.3 Data we do not collect

We do not collect payment details, government identification numbers, or sensitive personal data as defined under the IT Rules 2011, through this website.

3. How we use your data

We use personal data you provide solely for the purposes for which it was given:

  • To deliver the Owner's Profit Control Playbook to the email address you provided
  • To send you Veritas Insights articles when published, if you subscribed
  • To respond to enquiries sent to us by email
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the website is used (Google Analytics, only if you accept analytics cookies; see Section 5)

We do not use your personal data for advertising, profiling, automated decision-making, or any purpose beyond direct communication with you and the aggregate website analytics described above.

3.1 Legal basis

Under the DPDP Act 2023, the legal basis for processing your data is your explicit consent, given at the point of form submission. You may withdraw this consent at any time by contacting us.

For analytics cookies, the legal basis is your consent, given by clicking “Accept analytics” in the cookie banner. You may withdraw it at any time with Cookie settings.

3.2 Retention

We retain your email address for as long as you remain a subscriber or until you request removal. Enquiry emails are retained for up to 24 months for reference. We do not retain personal data beyond what is necessary for the stated purpose. Analytics data is retained by Google for the period configured in our Google Analytics account.

3.3 Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party for commercial purposes. Data may be processed by our form provider (FormSubmit) solely for delivery of form submissions. FormSubmit acts as a data processor on our behalf. If you accept analytics, Google LLC processes analytics data on our behalf as a service provider (see Section 6); this data may be transferred to and processed in the United States.

4. Your rights under the DPDP Act 2023

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have the following rights as a Data Principal:

  • Right to access — You may request a summary of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to correction — You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to erasure — You may request deletion of your personal data. We will comply unless retention is required by law.
  • Right to grievance redressal — You may raise a grievance with us and receive a response within a reasonable time.
  • Right to withdraw consent — You may withdraw consent for us to process your data at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal.
  • Right to nominate — You may nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these rights, write to us at vaishakh@veritashospitalityadvisors.in. We will respond within 30 days.

5. Cookies

This website uses Google Analytics (GA4), a web analytics service provided by Google, to understand how visitors use the site, for example which pages are viewed and how visitors arrive. Google Analytics is optional. The Google tag is present on every page but starts in a consent-denied state: it sets no analytics cookies unless you click “Accept analytics” in the cookie banner, and before you choose it may send only limited signals that do not use cookies. If you accept, it sets analytics cookies in your browser (for example _ga and _ga_ cookies) and collects data such as your IP address, browser and device type, and the pages you view. We use this information in aggregate to improve the site and do not use it to identify individual visitors.

Cookies set only if you accept analytics:

  • _ga — Google Analytics. Distinguishes visitors so we can produce aggregate statistics. Expires after 2 years.
  • _ga_X7QRDJ87V3 — Google Analytics 4. Keeps session state. Expires after 2 years.

Your choice itself is saved in your browser's local storage (key veritas_consent); it is not a cookie and is not sent to us or to Google. Advertising-related consent signals (ad storage, ad user data and ad personalisation) are always set to denied.

We do not use advertising cookies. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may also set technical cookies necessary for site delivery. These are not used for marketing.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with Cookie settings, block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's opt-out browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. Declining does not stop you from using this website. We store your choice in your browser (local storage) so we do not ask again on every visit.

The Hotel Profit Health Check calculator on this site operates entirely in your browser and stores no data on our servers.
