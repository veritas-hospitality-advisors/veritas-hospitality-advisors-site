01 — Service

Commercial Performance
reviewed from an owner's lens

Revenue strategy, pricing discipline, channel mix, market benchmarking, and demand segmentation — assessed independently, with no relationship to your revenue manager, OTA partner, or management company.

What Veritas reviews

Five areas where owner value is gained or lost

01

Pricing & Yield Strategy

Are rate decisions being made to maximise owner return — or to fill rooms? Veritas reviews your rate structure, season strategy, and yield decisions against market conditions and your own historical data. The question is not what your ADR is; it is what it should be.

02

Channel Mix & OTA Dependence

Each booking channel has a real cost the P&L rarely shows clearly. Veritas maps your channel contribution after commission, payment processing, and promotional spend — so you know what each booking actually earns, and where your exposure to OTA rate-parity agreements is highest.

03

Market & Competitor Benchmarking

How does your RevPAR, occupancy, and rate compare to the right competitive set — not the one your Compset Report was set up for? Veritas reviews your benchmarking methodology and ensures you are measuring yourself against properties owners and guests actually consider equivalent.

04

Demand Segmentation

Corporate, leisure, wholesale, and group segments carry different margins and different risk profiles. Veritas reviews your segment mix, identifies over-dependence on single sources, and recommends diversification strategies that improve both revenue quality and resilience.

05

Revenue Forecasting & Controls

Forecasting accuracy determines whether staffing, procurement, and capital allocation decisions are sound. Veritas reviews your forecasting process, the assumptions behind it, and the governance around who sees and challenges the numbers before decisions are made.

"Know what each booking channel earns after distribution costs. Improve RevPAR and topline quality."

The Veritas mandate

The owner's lens

Most revenue management is done in service of occupancy and ADR targets. Those metrics matter — but they are not the same as owner return. A night with 95% occupancy at a rate that barely covers distribution costs and displaces a higher-rated future booking is not a commercial success. Veritas brings an independent review layer that asks a question day-to-day operations rarely leave time for: is this strategy maximising your asset's return, or managing to a brand metric?

What this delivers for owners

Clearer numbers. Better decisions.

01 A clear view of true channel profitability — what each booking source earns after all costs
02 Rate and yield decisions anchored to owner return, not occupancy targets or brand compliance
03 Benchmarking that is honest about your real competitive set — not set up to make numbers look better
04 Segment diversification that reduces revenue risk and improves quality of earnings
05 Forecasting the owner can rely on — with proper governance around who challenges the assumptions
06 A monthly commercial review presented in language that supports owner decisions, not just hotel metrics

How Veritas engages

From diagnostic to active oversight

Commercial performance work begins with a structured baseline — understanding your current position before recommending anything. Most owners are surprised by what the baseline reveals about channel costs, rate erosion, and benchmarking gaps.

From there, Veritas moves into active oversight: monthly review of commercial decisions, regular benchmarking updates, and clear reporting back to the owner on what is working and what needs to change.

Execution stays with your property team and revenue management. Veritas is the independent layer that ensures the owner sees the picture clearly — and that decisions are made against an owner-approved plan.

Stage 1 · Days 0–30

Diagnostic & Baseline

Channel profitability mapping. Rate strategy review. Competitive set calibration. Demand segmentation analysis. Forecasting process assessment. Delivered as a structured review with clear findings and a prioritised action roadmap.

Stage 2 · Day 30 onwards

Active Commercial Oversight

Monthly commercial performance review. Rate and yield strategy input. Channel mix monitoring. Benchmarking updates. Revenue forecast review. Owner-facing reporting on topline quality and commercial discipline.
