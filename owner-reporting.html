Owner Reporting
& asset value

Revenue dashboards, monthly performance summaries, GOP analysis, and clear action plans — in language that supports owner decisions, not just hotel metrics. No surprises. No blind spots.

The reporting problem

Most hotel reporting is designed for hotels, not owners

The reports that come from your hotel — occupancy percentages, ADR, RevPAR, cover counts — are designed to satisfy brand compliance requirements and give management teams performance benchmarks. They are not designed to answer the questions an owner needs answered.

What is my asset actually earning? Where is GOP leaking and why? What decisions do I need to make this month, and what are the options? Is my hotel on track against the plan we agreed?

What owners typically cannot see

True net revenue after all distribution and OTA costs
GOP with a clear bridge from revenue to profit — not a management summary
Variance explanations that go beyond "market conditions"
Which decisions need to be made now, and what the options actually are
Forward-looking indicators — not just last month's results
How the asset is performing against its long-term value trajectory

What Veritas delivers

Four reporting layers, one clear picture

01

Periodic Revenue Dashboards

Weekly and monthly revenue performance presented in owner-facing format — occupancy, rate, RevPAR, channel mix, and net revenue after distribution. Benchmarked against prior period, prior year, and the competitive set. Designed for a 10-minute review, not a 2-hour management meeting.

02

Monthly Performance Summaries

A structured monthly report that answers three questions: what happened, why it happened, and what needs to happen next. Written in owner language, not hotel jargon. Includes commercial performance, operational highlights, and a clear list of decisions required from the owner.

03

GOP Analysis & Bridge

Gross operating profit is the number that matters most to an independent hotel owner — but it is also the number most easily obscured by reporting conventions. Veritas produces a clean GOP bridge each month: from revenue to profit, with every major cost line explained and variance from budget and prior year clearly accounted for.

04

Clear Action Plans

Every monthly report concludes with a prioritised action list: what Veritas recommends, what the property team is responsible for executing, and what the owner needs to decide. No ambiguity about who is doing what, and no management recommendations that quietly require owner capital without being flagged as such.

The Veritas reporting principle

Reporting should create accountability, not manage it. When a report is designed by the people being reported on, it will inevitably emphasise what went well and soften what did not. Veritas reporting is produced by an independent party, for the owner — which means it can say clearly when something is not working, why, and what needs to change.

What this delivers for owners

Visibility. Accountability. Control.

03 Revenue performance benchmarked against the competitive set that actually matters to your asset

How Veritas produces owner reporting

Built from the property's own data — reviewed independently

Veritas works directly with your hotel's data — PMS exports, F&B reports, cost reports, and bank reconciliations where relevant. The reporting is not built on what the property team chooses to highlight; it is built on the underlying numbers.

The first month establishes the baseline framework: what to report, at what frequency, in what format, and against what benchmarks. That framework is agreed with the owner before reporting begins — so there are no surprises about what gets disclosed.

From Month 2 onwards, reporting is structured and regular. The owner receives a periodic dashboard and a monthly summary, with an optional review call to walk through key decisions.

Stage 1 · Days 0–30

Reporting Framework Setup

Data access established. Reporting format and frequency agreed with owner. Competitive benchmarking set calibrated. GOP bridge methodology aligned. First owner report produced covering the baseline period — your starting point before any improvement work begins.

Stage 2 · Monthly thereafter

Ongoing Owner Reporting

Weekly revenue dashboard. Monthly performance summary with GOP bridge. Action plan with clear owner decisions. Quarterly review of reporting framework and benchmarks. Annual asset value assessment available as add-on.
